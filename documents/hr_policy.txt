Parental Leave: Employees are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Leave requests should be submitted to HR at least 30 days in advance when possible.

Sick Leave: Employees accrue 1 sick day per month, up to a maximum of 10 days per year. Unused sick days do not carry over to the next calendar year.

Performance Reviews: Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and December. Employees receive written feedback and set goals with their manager during each review.
